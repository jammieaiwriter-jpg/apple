1. **Public publisher-oriented catalogues:** [Junyi / 類康軒](https://www.junyiacademy.org/topics/k-m1a), [類翰林](https://www.junyiacademy.org/topics/h-m1a). It does not establish 108–113 edition continuity.
3. **Local tcool manifests:** prove downloaded-paper publisher/grade/year labels only. They are intentionally not used to invent chapter titles or historic edition mappings.
4. **Existing curriculum/math-g1..g4-school.json:** used only to form provisional stable-SkillID bridges. It is not evidence of a publisher’s original headings.
